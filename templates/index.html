Operational Guidelines

Follow these steps for optimal AI detection accuracy.

  • Allow Camera Access
    When prompted by your browser, click Allow. Detection runs entirely on-device — no video is sent to any server.
  • Face the Camera
    Keep your face clearly visible. Good lighting and a centred face improve detection accuracy.
  • Audio Alert System
    An audio alarm plays automatically when prolonged eye closure is detected. Keep your volume on.
  • Event Logging
    Each detected drowsiness event is logged to the server database with a timestamp and EAR value.
